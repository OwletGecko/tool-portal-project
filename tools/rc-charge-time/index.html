RC charge-time calculator

Enter a resistance and a capacitance, and choose the unit for each. The calculator gives the time constant and how long the capacitor takes to charge.

How it works

A capacitor stores electric charge, like a small tank. A resistor in front of it limits how fast the tank fills. The time constant (τ, the Greek letter tau) tells you how fast: τ = R × C. After one τ the capacitor has charged to about 63% of the supply voltage. Charging slows down as it fills, because there is less "room" left, so it takes about 2.3τ to reach 90% and about 4.6τ to reach 99%.
