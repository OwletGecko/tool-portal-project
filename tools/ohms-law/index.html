Ohm's law calculator

Fill in any two boxes and leave the third empty. The calculator works out the missing value and the power.

How it works

Ohm's law says that voltage (V) equals current (I) times resistance (R): V = I × R. Voltage is the "push" on the electric charge, current is how much charge flows, and resistance is how much the part resists the flow. Power (P, in watts) is how much energy is used each second: P = V × I.
